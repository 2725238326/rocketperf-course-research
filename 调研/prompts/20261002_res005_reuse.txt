RES-005 research, cutoff 2026-10-02. Select at least two computable variable-condition, cooling, reuse, or improvement questions for a liquid rocket engine course project. Prefer NASA NTRS, DLR, manufacturer/developer, and peer-reviewed sources. For each question provide: (1) a zero-dimensional or quasi-one-dimensional formulation; (2) data available in this project versus explicit assumptions; (3) benefit metric, cost/constraint, and validation method; (4) what cannot be inferred about real lifetime or control. Candidate topics include nozzle area ratio versus ambient pressure, turbine branch exhaust utilization, throttling performance boundaries, and cooling heat pickup. Do not provide manufacturing or control implementation details. Do not treat one recovery success, a design reuse target, or a steady-state scenario as lifetime proof. Give title, author or institution, date, URL, and a short supporting quote, distinguishing primary sources from search summaries. About 1500 Chinese characters; conclude with a recommendation for freezing the course project's main topic.
