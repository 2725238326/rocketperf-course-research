## 推荐阅读顺序

1. **先看任务和边界**：[`worknow.md`](../worknow.md) → [`rules.md`](../rules.md) → [`handoff.md`](../handoff.md)。需要完整契约时再看[`docs/tasks.md`](../docs/tasks.md)。
2. **理解项目地图**：[`README.md`](../README.md) → [`docs/README.md`](../docs/README.md) → [`docs/project-map.md`](../docs/project-map.md)。入口页负责指路，不重复维护实时进度。
3. **理解两型四级段**：[`docs/two-vehicle-study.md`](../docs/two-vehicle-study.md) → [`data/parameters/vehicle_cycle_evidence.json`](../data/parameters/vehicle_cycle_evidence.json) → [`调研/证据与参数台账.md`](../调研/证据与参数台账.md)。这些文件分开记录事实、家族证据、假设和未知，不把机器映射直接当实机输入。
4. **理解循环实现**：[`docs/cycle-integration.md`](../docs/cycle-integration.md) → [`docs/engine-cycle-validation.md`](../docs/engine-cycle-validation.md) → [`docs/cycle-validation.md`](../docs/cycle-validation.md)。前两份说明新接口/方程和正式复核，后一份说明历史给定热状态外排模型。
5. **复跑新循环**：先按[`docs/receiving.md`](../docs/receiving.md)确认环境，再运行 `python tools/quality.py`、`python tools/engine_cycles.py verify results/research/engine_cycles_v2_20261013`，最后使用 `cases/vehicles/` 中的指定输入演示。结果只按 manifest 的输入和哈希解释。
6. **准备汇报材料**：先读[`deliverables/report.md`](../deliverables/report.md)的研究叙述，再读[`deliverables/presentation.md`](../deliverables/presentation.md)的讲解顺序；二者都必须保留“研究情景、不代表实机性能”的口径。
